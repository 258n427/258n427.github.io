## ✨ Key Features

PSXS5 is more than a bare emulator core. It bundles a set of quality-of-life features that make the experience feel like a polished console app rather than a technical tool.

### 🖼️ Cover-Flow Game Shelf

Your games are displayed as a scrolling, three-dimensional shelf of box art — the same style of browsing you would find on a modern console storefront. Games flow past as you move left and right, letting you recognize titles by their artwork instead of reading filenames. It is faster, friendlier, and far more enjoyable than digging through a plain list.

### 🔍 xBR Upscaling

The original PlayStation rendered games at a low resolution designed for CRT televisions. On a modern 4K display, those pixels can look blocky and harsh. PSXS5 includes xBR upscaling, a smoothing technique that intelligently blends edges so sprites and text appear cleaner while keeping the original art style intact. The result is a picture that looks sharp on a big screen without turning into a blurry mess.

### 🏆 RetroAchievements

RetroAchievements support is built in. Connect your account and every compatible game can award trophies for the things you actually did in them — beating a boss, finding a secret, finishing a run under a time limit. It adds a layer of challenge and replay value to games you may have finished a dozen times before.

### 🧰 Cheat Codes

A cheats panel is available directly from the in-game menu. Enable codes for extra lives, unlimited continues, unlocked content, or other tweaks without needing to hunt down external tools or patch files. Cheats can be toggled on and off at any time, so you can use them to get past a tough section and then switch back to a normal playthrough.

### 💾 Save States and Memory Cards

Save your progress at any moment with save states, or keep things authentic with virtual memory cards. Both systems work side by side, so you can preserve a quick moment before a difficult boss and still maintain a traditional save file for long-term progress.

## ❓ Common Questions

**Do I need to install anything else first?** PSXS5 runs as homebrew, so your console needs to be jailbroken with a homebrew loader such as etaHEN available. That is the only prerequisite.

**Where do my game files go?** Any folder your console can read works — a USB drive is the simplest choice. Point PSXS5 at that folder during setup and your library will populate.

**How do I update PSXS5?** Return to the releases page, download the newest build, and replace the older copy on your USB drive. Your saves and settings stay where they are.

**Will my existing saves carry over?** Yes. Save states and memory card files live alongside the application, so updating the emulator does not erase your progress.

**Why does a game look slightly different from how I remember it?** xBR upscaling changes how edges are drawn. If you prefer the original crisp pixel look, switch the display mode in settings.
